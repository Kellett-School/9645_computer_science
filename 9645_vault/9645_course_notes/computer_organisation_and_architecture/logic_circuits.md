The combination of [[logic_gates]] to create a whole circuit that performs some kind of functionality

## Example:

The logic circuit for the [[logic_statements|logic statement]]:
$$X=\overline{A \cdot B} + B \cdot \overline A$$
Would look like this:
![[Excalidraw/logic_circuit_examples.md#^group=hAqBJgz8FxWCZ9JUritlV|logic_X_EQ_NOT_A_AND_B_OR_NOT_A_AND_B]]

- **Dots** represent connections between lines (wires)
- **Lines** (wires) are always drawn with straight edges
- Logic Circuits usually go from **inputs** on the left to outputs on the **right** (note that this is different to convention for [[logic_statements]] which usually have outputs (result) on the left)

## Simplification of Logic Circuits
